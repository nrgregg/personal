# Fish Sprite_2 page accents

Sampled from the original sprite's first frame, `sprite_00.png`.

| Color | Hex | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grey (light accent) | `#6E8988` | Home |
| Pink | `#D6203B` | Research Blog and Archive |
| Green | `#27B481` | Projects |
| Yellow | `#DDB210` | Further Reading |

The active tab uses its page color as an underline. Non-Home pages also have a matching 5px top border on their main panel (the first panel on multi-panel pages). Archive shares the Research Blog accent.

Edit `site/template.html` for styles; rebuild `index.html` through the existing site build command.
